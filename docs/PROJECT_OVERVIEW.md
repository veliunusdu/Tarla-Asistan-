# PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1. About the Project

### Project Name

Tarla Asistanı (Farm Assistant)

### Project Purpose

Tarla Asistanı is an AI-supported digital farm management system that enables farmers to make daily agricultural decisions more accurately, systematically, and easily.

The system integrates:

- Farm information,
- Crop growth stages,
- Weather conditions,
- Past operations,
- User observations,
- Expert evaluations,

to generate daily tasks for the farmer.

Our primary goal is:

> "To ensure that when the farmer opens the app every morning, they can see the most important tasks they need to do that day."

---

## 2. Problem Statement

### Problems Experienced by Farmers

- Daily farm operations cannot be tracked systematically.
- Completed operations are not recorded.
- Weather information cannot be directly converted into agricultural decisions.
- Disease and pest problems may be noticed too late.
- Rapid access to expert support is unavailable.
- Existing applications can be overly technical and complex.
- Farm history cannot be stored systematically.
- Farmers dislike long and complex data entry.
- Agricultural information is scattered across many different applications.

### Problems Experienced by Agronomists (Agricultural Engineers)

- Information received from farmers can be incomplete.
- Photos, weather information, and past operations cannot be viewed on a single screen.
- Whether the given recommendations are implemented cannot be tracked.
- It can be difficult to track a large number of farmers at the same time.

## 4. Value Proposition

Tarla Asistanı provides:

### For Farmers

- Generates daily tasks.
- Notifies about weather risks.
- Records completed activities.
- Facilitates access to expert support.
- Systematically stores farm history.
- Offers voice input support.

### For Agronomists

- Prioritizes incoming cases.
- Shows farm history on a single screen.
- Enables task creation.
- Facilitates communication between farmer and expert.
- Enables systematic tracking of more farmers.

### Product Promise

> "Shows the three most important tasks for your farm every morning."

## 8. Core Principles of the Project

1. Farmers will be shown actionable tasks rather than technical data.

2. The number of daily tasks will be kept as low as possible.

3. Artificial intelligence will not replace expert support.

4. Unconfirmed results will not be presented as definitive.

5. Risky decisions will be escalated to the agronomist.

6. Voice input will be one of the priority features.

7. Basic features will be usable even with low internet connectivity.

8. New features will not be added before the MVP scope is validated.

---

## 9. One-Sentence Product Definition

> Tarla Asistanı is a digital farm management application that shows the farmer the most important tasks to do in their field every day, easily records completed activities, and enables them to reach an agronomist when needed.
